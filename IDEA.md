# My app

**Domain:** Student spending tracker

**User story:** As a college student, I need to pick a month and see my budget, how much I've spent, and what's left, so that I know where my money is going.

**What the user types or picks:**

- Month (pick a month to review)
- Amount (type a number, like 12.50)
- Category (pick from a list: Bills, Groceries, Shopping, Eating Out, Entertainment, Other)
- Date (pick from a calendar, defaults to today)
- Note (optional, type a short description, like "boba with friends")
